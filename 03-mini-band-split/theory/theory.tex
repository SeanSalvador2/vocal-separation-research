\begin{abstract}
This note derives the mathematics implemented in \code{singnet/} +
\code{scripts/} for Direction 03: band-splitting as a change of weight-sharing
scope; the HTK-mel band edges for our STFT pins; the boundary/receptive-field
analysis behind a possible negative result; and --- the crux --- the exact
parameter and compute accounting, giving the closed form $P(c)$, the solved width
$c=23$ with a single bottleneck bump to $b_5=382$, the per-module tables that
match \code{scripts/match\_params.py} and the unit tests, and the FLOPs derivation
showing the variants are $\approx\!0.54\times$ the baseline's encoder compute at
matched parameters (capacity $\ne$ compute). It closes with the per-band metrics
and the three-arm ordered-chain statistics. Every number in \S4 is machine-checked
against the built \code{nn.Module}.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Notation.} A mono mixture chunk has magnitude $|X|\in\mathbb
R_{\ge0}^{F\times T}$ on $F=2048$ network bins, $T=256$ frames. A magnitude-mask
U-Net predicts $M\in[0,1]^{F\times T}$; $\widehat V=M\odot|X|e^{i\angle X}$,
$\hat v=\iSTFT(\widehat V)$. Direction 03 changes only the encoder front-end: one
full-spectrum encoder (baseline \code{SingNetC1}) vs.\ $B=3$ per-band towers.

\section{The band-split idea, formalized}

A 2-D convolution is translation-equivariant along frequency: the baseline applies
one filter bank $W$ to \emph{all} bins,
$(\mathcal E_{\text{base}}x)[:,f,t]=\phi(W*x)[:,f,t]$ for every $f$
(full-spectrum weight sharing). The band-split encoder partitions $[0,F)$ into
contiguous bands $\mathcal B_b=[e_b,e_{b+1})$ and gives each its own operator,
\[
(\mathcal E_{\text{split}}x)[:,f,t]=\phi\big(W_b*x_{\mathcal B_b}\big)[:,f,t],
\qquad f\in\mathcal B_b,
\]
band-local weight sharing: each band's statistics get dedicated capacity. We keep
exactly the partition + per-band conv capacity and \emph{drop} the cross-band
sequence model of BSRNN/Mel-RoFormer --- dropping it is what isolates the
partition (otherwise a win could be the attention). The towers use the
\emph{same} 5-level block design as the baseline, so the only change is the
weight-sharing scope; no cross-band mixing occurs before the bottleneck.

\section{Mel scale and band edges}

The HTK mel scale is $m(f)=2595\log_{10}(1+f/700)$, inverse
$f(m)=700(10^{m/2595}-1)$. An equal-mel partition of $[0,f_{\max}]$ into $B$ bands
puts interior edges at $m_i=\tfrac iB m(f_{\max})$, mapped to Hz and then to the
nearest bin $k(f)=\round(f\,n_{\text{fft}}/sr)$. For $sr=44100$,
$n_{\text{fft}}=4096$ ($f_{\max}=22050$, $F=2048$),
$m(f_{\max})=2595\log_{10}(32.5)=3923.34$, so $f_1=1533.9$\,Hz $\to k_1=142$ and
$f_2=6428.9$\,Hz $\to k_2=597$:
\[
\text{mel: } [0,142,597,2048]\ (\approx1.53,6.43\,\text{kHz}),\qquad
\text{uniform: } [0,683,1365,2048]\ (\approx7.35,14.70\,\text{kHz}).
\]
The mel layout concentrates two of three bands below $6.43$\,kHz, matching where
vocal energy lives ($\sim100$\,Hz--$4$\,kHz). Caveat: MUSDB18 is AAC-limited to
$\approx16$\,kHz, so the mel top band $[6.43,22.05]$\,kHz is partly dead spectrum
--- a property of mel spacing on this data, stated in advance and NaN-guarded by
the banded metrics.

\section{Boundary analysis}

Each band of width $w_b$ is zero-padded on its high-frequency side to
$p_b=\lceil w_b/32\rceil\cdot32$ (so five stride-2 layers halve exactly). For both
layouts the padded total is $P=\sum_b p_b=2112$ (mel $p=(160,480,1472)$, uniform
$(704,704,704)$); $P$ is carried through the network and the final mask is cropped
back to $2048$ bins by a fixed, exactly-invertible gather. A tower has the
baseline receptive field ($r_\ell=5,13,29,61,125$ bins) but is \emph{confined to
its band}: structure straddling an edge (e.g.\ a harmonic near the mel edge
$k_1=142$) is split between two towers that cannot share it in the encoder,
whereas the baseline sees it freely. This severing is the mechanism for a
pre-registered \emph{refuted (negative)} outcome; cross-band information returns
only in the shared decoder, and the per-band figure localizes damage at
band-boundary-adjacent bands.

\section{Parameter and compute accounting (the crux)}

\paragraph{Spatial-size independence.} A conv/transpose-conv $(c_{\text{in}},
c_{\text{out}},5\times5)$ has $25c_{\text{in}}c_{\text{out}}+c_{\text{out}}$
parameters for \emph{any} spatial size; its BatchNorm adds $2c_{\text{out}}$. One
conv+BN block costs $b(c_{\text{in}},c_{\text{out}})=25c_{\text{in}}c_{\text{out}}
+3c_{\text{out}}$. Consequently the mel and uniform variants --- differing only in
band widths --- have the \emph{identical} parameter count.

\paragraph{Closed form.} With base width $c$ and bottleneck $b_5$,
\begin{align*}
E(c,b_5)&=b(1,c)+b(c,2c)+b(2c,4c)+b(4c,8c)+b(8c,b_5)=1050c^2+70c+200c\,b_5+3b_5,\\
D(c,b_5)&=b(b_5,8c)+b(16c,4c)+b(8c,2c)+b(4c,c)+b(2c,c)=2150c^2+48c+200c\,b_5,
\end{align*}
and the variant (three towers, one decoder, head $c+1$) is
\[
\boxed{\,P(c,b_5)=3E(c,b_5)+D(c,b_5)+(c+1)=5300c^2+259c+800c\,b_5+9b_5+1.\,}
\]
At $b_5=16c$ this is $P_{\text{pure}}(c)=18100c^2+403c+1$; the baseline (one
encoder+decoder+head at $c=32$) is $9{,}835{,}745=P_0$.

\paragraph{Width selection.} $P_{\text{pure}}(c)=P_0\Rightarrow c\approx23.31$.
Neither pure integer width is within $2\%$: $P_{\text{pure}}(23)=9{,}584{,}170$
($-2.558\%$), $P_{\text{pure}}(24)=10{,}435{,}273$ ($+6.095\%$). Rule: take the
largest $c$ under budget ($c=23$), then the single positive bottleneck bump
$b_5=16c+\Delta$ minimizing $|P-P_0|$. As $P$ is linear in $b_5$ with slope
$800c+9=18409$,
\[
\Delta=\Big\lfloor\tfrac{251{,}575}{18409}\Big\rceil=14\Rightarrow b_5=382,\qquad
P(23,382)=9{,}841{,}896\ (+0.0625\%).
\]

\paragraph{Compute: capacity $\ne$ compute.} A conv layer's MACs scale with output
spatial size, $\text{MACs}_\ell\approx S_{\text{out},\ell}\cdot25c_{\text{in}}
c_{\text{out}}$. The baseline encoder runs on $F=2048$; the variant runs three
towers each on $\approx F/3$, and the $3\times$ count cancels the $1/3$ extent
($\sum_b p_b/2^\ell=P/2^\ell$), so the variant encoder is like a \emph{single}
width-$c$ encoder on the padded spectrum:
\[
\frac{\text{MACs}^{\text{enc}}_{\text{variant}}}{\text{MACs}^{\text{enc}}_{\text{base}}}
\approx\Big(\tfrac{c}{32}\Big)^2\frac{P}{2048}
=\Big(\tfrac{23}{32}\Big)^2\tfrac{2112}{2048}\approx0.53,
\]
and $=0.541$ exactly with $b_5=382$ (identical for both variants, as it depends
only on $P$). Whole-model MACs $\approx0.54\times$ baseline. Thus at matched
\emph{parameters} the variants are $\approx1.85\times$ cheaper in encoder compute
--- an incidental bonus reported as a descriptive secondary, never the claim.
Three towers hold $3\times$ the per-encoder parameters yet do $1\times$ the spatial
work at a smaller width, hence half the compute.

\section{Per-band mechanism metrics}

For an analysis band $b=[\underline f,\overline f)$, band-limited SI-SDR zeros all
bins outside $b$ in both signals' own STFTs, inverts, and scores SI-SDR ---
well-defined (a common linear band mask, no phase asymmetry), NaN-guarded for empty
or silent-reference bands. Band magnitude error is
$\mean_{(k,t)\in b}||R|-|E||\,/\,\mean_{(k,t)\in b}|R|$, a relative L1 error,
NaN when the reference band level is below $\varepsilon$. Analysis uses a fixed
6-band grid --- the union of both variants' interior edges, a $100$\,Hz floor
split and $[0,sr/2]$ --- so neither arm is scored on its own favorable grid; the
mechanism figure plots per-band (variant $-$ baseline) deltas, expected to
concentrate in the vocal-dense bands under a positive headline.

\section{Statistics of the design}

The three-arm noise band is
$\sigma_{\text{seed}}=\sqrt{\tfrac13(s^2_{\text{base}}+s^2_{\text{uni}}
+s^2_{\text{mel}})}$. H-03 is the ordered chain $\val(\text{mel})>\val(\text{uni})
>\val(\text{base})$, tested by $E_1=\val(\text{uni})-\val(\text{base})$ and
$E_2=\val(\text{mel})-\val(\text{uni})$ against $\sigma_{\text{seed}}$. The two
links are \emph{jointly} pre-registered as one ordered claim, so no
multiple-comparison correction is applied; the honesty comes from enumerating the
partial cells (E1-only, E2-only, refuted-null, refuted-negative) \emph{in
advance}:
\[
\text{verdict}=\begin{cases}
\text{fully supported}& E_1>\sigma\wedge E_2>\sigma,\\
\text{partial }E_1& E_1>\sigma\wedge E_2\le\sigma,\\
\text{partial }E_2& E_2>\sigma\wedge E_1\le\sigma,\\
\text{refuted (negative)}& \text{neither},\ \val(\text{base})-\val(\text{uni})>\sigma\wedge\val(\text{base})-\val(\text{mel})>\sigma,\\
\text{refuted (null)}& \text{neither, otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\]
Matched seeds do not match init across arms (different shapes), so seed matching
controls data order only; the 3-seed spread absorbs init variance. A FULL-budget
pair (best variant + baseline) guards budget-dependence: if it reverses the
REDUCED sign, the verdict downgrades to \emph{mixed (budget-dependent)}.
